## 💡 Tips for text-heavy posters

1. **Put literal text in quotes in the prompt** — `Headline reads: "Ship faster."`
2. **Use ALL CAPS for tricky words** — brands, acronyms, uncommon words
3. **Spell out brand/product names letter-by-letter** if they keep 
   getting mangled: `The product name is "F-O-R-G-E" (FORGE)`
4. **Always add constraints**:
   - `"Render text verbatim. No extra words. No duplicate text."`
5. **Iterate**: if first output has wrong font weight, edit with 
   `"make the headline heavier/thinner, keep everything else"`
